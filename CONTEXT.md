# SplitBill domain

This context describes shared expenses and the balances of group members. Terms come from the take-home brief.

## Language

**User**: A person identified in requests by the `X-User` name.

**Group**: A collection of members who share expenses.

**Member**: A user who belongs to a particular group.

**Expense**: An immutable shared cost recorded in a group, with a payer, an amount and participant shares.

**Payer**: The group member who paid an expense.

**Share**: The part of an expense allocated to a member.

**Equal split**: Allocation of an expense across an ordered list of members, with remaining satang assigned in that order.

**Exact split**: Allocation of an expense using specified member shares whose total equals the expense amount.

**Balance**: A member's net position within a group; a positive balance means money is owed to that member.

**Satang**: One hundredth of a Thai baht.
